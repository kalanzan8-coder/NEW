FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects launch in six weeks from kickoff. Strategy takes the first week, design and build run in sprints for four, and the final week is launch prep and handover.

What does a project cost?

Every project is fixed scope and fixed price, agreed before we start. After the discovery call we send a single quote — no hourly billing, no retainers, no surprise invoices.

Do I need an existing audience?

It helps, but it's not required. During discovery we assess your reach and build the launch plan around it — whether that's an email list of 50,000 or a cold start.

What happens after launch?

You own everything — the product, the funnel, the content and the accounts. We hand over full documentation, and you can book follow-on sprints whenever you want to grow further.
