What we do

The gap between knowing your craft and selling it.

You're good at something. People already trust you for it. But knowing your craft and turning it into a product people pay for are two completely different skills, and the gap between them is where most experts get stuck.

That's the gap we close. Vorgrow is an info-product agency that takes you from "I should probably make a course" to a finished, launched product with an audience ready to buy it. We don't hand you a template and wish you luck. We sit on your side of the table and build the whole thing with you: we figure out what your audience actually wants, shape it into a product worth paying for, build the platform and pages it lives on, and run the launch that gets it in front of people.

You bring the expertise. We bring the system that turns it into a business.

How it works

A clear process, built around your launch.

Around six weeks from kickoff to drop day. Five steps, clear deliverables at every one, so you always know what is happening and what is next.

Step 01 — Week 1

Discover

A free strategy call to understand your audience, your expertise, and what you want to build. You leave with a clear written proposal: scope, timeline, and price range, no guesswork.

  • 45-minute discovery call
  • Written proposal and roadmap

Step 02 — Weeks 1 to 2

Validate

Before a page is built, we find out what your audience will actually pay for. We survey your following, mine your expertise for the offer, and map the funnel from a low-ticket entry point to your flagship product.

  • Audience survey and demand check
  • Offer and funnel mapped end to end

Step 03 — Weeks 2 to 5

Build

We turn your knowledge into a real product: structured modules, recorded lessons, a clean platform, and conversion-focused landing pages. Designed to look premium and built to be finished by the people who buy it.

  • Course, platform, and pages built
  • Weekly check-ins, no surprises

Step 04 — Week 6

Launch

We warm your audience for weeks with genuinely valuable free content, then run a coordinated drop day with emails, posts, and offers firing together, so you launch to people who already trust you instead of an empty room.

  • Pre-launch nurture and lead magnets
  • One coordinated drop day

Step 05 — Ongoing

Grow

You own everything: the product, the funnel, the content, and the rights. We hand over a system you can keep selling, relaunch, and build on, with follow-on sprints whenever you want to push further.

  • Full handover and documentation
  • Relaunch and expansion sprints

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a typical project take?

Around six weeks from kickoff to launch, though it flexes with the size of the product. Larger courses or more complex funnels take longer, so we'll give you a realistic timeline before we start, and you'll know what's happening at every stage.

What does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of your product and funnel. The best way to get an accurate number is a quick call. We'll learn what you're building and put together a clear proposal with no surprises.

Do I need an existing audience?

It helps, but it's not a dealbreaker. A big part of our process is building your free-value email list and warming an audience before launch. If you already have a following, we'll put it to work; if you're earlier on, we'll help you build the engine.

What happens after launch?

You own everything: content, files, pages, and rights. We hand over a product and a system you can keep selling, relaunch, and build on.
